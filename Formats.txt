Core Plugin and Master Formats.esm (Master File): The main game database (FalloutNV.esm) and DLCs. It holds foundational world cells, landscapes, and records..esp (Plugin/Mod): Standard mod files that layer changes, items, or quests on top of master files..nam (Text List): Small text files used by the root directory to designate active official DLC packages.Asset Archive and Media Formats.bsa (Bethesda Softworks Archive): Compressed container files holding bundled textures, meshes, sounds, and voices (e.g., Fallout - Textures.bsa)..nif (NetImmerse 3D Model): The mesh format for items, characters, armor, and world architecture..dds (DirectDraw Surface): Texture format used for skin, environment, and item graphics..ogg / .wav: Audio compression formats used for in-game radio, voice acting, and sound effects.
