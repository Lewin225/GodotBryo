FLST — Form List record

AUTHORED ROLE
FLST is an ordered list of FormID references. For equipment, ARMO.BIPL points
to an FLST whose LNAM entries identify the ARMA records that make up the armor's
biped model list.

EQUIPMENT CENSUS
All 115 ARMO.BIPL references found in the base FalloutNV.esm resolve to FLST
records. Expanding them yields 247 LNAM entries: 243 ARMA entries, one ARMO
entry, and three unresolved references when the base ESM is considered alone.
The unresolved entries must retain their original FormIDs and be diagnosed by
load order rather than silently discarded.

Across all ten official masters, 142 ARMO.BIPL references resolve to FLST and
expand to 301 LNAM entries. 300 entries resolve to ARMA and one remains
unresolved in the combined official-master set.

RESOURCE MODEL
FLST should become a typed BipedModelListResource when consumed by ARMO, while
remaining a general FormListResource for other systems. The equipment-specific
consumer should resolve entries by FormID and verify the target record type.
