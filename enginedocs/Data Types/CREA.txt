CREA — Creature base record

ROLE
CREA is authored creature-base data. ACRE supplies a placed creature
instance. CREA is an actor record, but it is not an NPC_ record and does not
carry the NPC_ RACE-linked human appearance contract.

FRESH ESM CENSUS
The official master files in `fresh_nv_copy/Data/` were scanned directly with
the native ESM reader on 2026-09-10. The census covered 2,238 CREA records:

- FalloutNV.esm: 1,578
- DeadMoney.esm: 77
- HonestHearts.esm: 96
- LonesomeRoad.esm: 141
- OldWorldBlues.esm: 346
- CaravanPack.esm, ClassicPack.esm, GunRunnersArsenal.esm,
  MercenaryPack.esm, and TribalPack.esm: 0

Observed across those records:

- EDID, ACBS, AIDT, DATA, BNAM, EAMT, NAM4, NAM5, NIFT, OBND, PNAM, RNAM,
  TNAM, and WNAM: 2,238/2,238
- CNTO inventory entries: 3,035 total occurrences
- NIFZ model-list subrecord: 1,573/2,238
- MODL: 1,668/2,238
- KFFZ animation-file list: 202/2,238
- PKID AI-package links: 3,470 total occurrences
- SPLO spell links: 1,893 total occurrences
- SNAM faction entries: 4,071 total occurrences
- SCRI script links: 473/2,238

These are raw observations. They include records inherited from templates and
records with optional fields absent; they do not imply that every field is
currently decoded by this project.

CONFIRMED APPEARANCE AND ANIMATION SHAPE
CREA has explicit model-list data. NIFZ contains one or more NIF filenames;
the census found examples containing multiple filenames. MODL is also present on many records and
is commonly a creature skeleton path in the shipped data. KFFZ is an explicit
list of KF filenames on a subset of CREA records, so creature animation data
cannot be assumed to be the human `Characters\\_Male` animation set.

The GECK Creature window exposes ModelList, Animation, Blood, Sounds,
Destruction, Inventory, Scripts, and AI-related tabs. These are consistent
with the observed CREA subrecords and establish that creatures share actor
composition concerns such as inventory, animation, scripts, and
gore-related data.

WHAT CREA DOES NOT CONFIRM
No NPC-style race link was found. RNAM occurs on all 2,238 records, but in the
CREA layout it is the impact-data-set field and must not be interpreted as an
NPC race FormID. CREA therefore must not be routed through NPC RACE/HAIR/EYES
or FaceGen assembly by default.

PNAM occurs on all 2,238 records, but its meaning and relationship to the
creature ModelList remain undecoded in this project. It is recorded here as
raw observed data rather than being promoted to a guessed appearance contract.
Likewise, the exact rule connecting each CREA.MODL skeleton path to the NIFZ
model list still needs to be implemented from these explicit fields; no
filename-convention fallback should be introduced without a separate census.

RUNTIME MODEL
Creature and NPC nodes should share the actor-level implementation for
inventory, equipment attachment, animation playback, root motion, scripts,
and common actor state where their assets support those operations. They need
separate appearance assembly leaves: NPC uses RACE/HAIR/EYES/FaceGen, while
CREA uses its own MODL/NIFZ/KFFZ/PNAM data. Creature-specific embedded weapons
and weapon-list behavior belong to the creature path, even when they reuse
the common equipment renderer.
