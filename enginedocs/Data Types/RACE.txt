RACE — Race record

ROLE
RACE supplies the race-level appearance collections used by NPC_ records. Its
body and head-part collections are gender-specific and use real indexed entries.

RESOLVED DATA
- Male and female body-part descriptors.
- Male and female head-part descriptors.
- Each descriptor preserves its RACE index, model path, and icon/texture path.
- Icon-only entries, such as ears in the verified human data, are preserved without
  fabricating a model path.

RUNTIME MODEL
RACE becomes a RaceRecord resource. NpcRecord.race points to that typed resource;
the NPC node selects the collection matching the NPC's gender.

RACE does not make GamebryoActor inherit from ContainerNode. It is appearance data,
not a runtime node superclass.
