ARMO — Armor record

AUTHORED ROLE
ARMO is the item-level armor record. It owns the armor's value, weight, armor
ratings, equipment type, effects/scripts, sounds, and the biped model list used
to assemble its visual armor addons.

CONFIRMED FIELDS
- BMDT: 8-byte biped data; its first uint32 is the biped-slot flag mask and the
  remaining bytes are general/unused data whose complete meaning is not assumed.
- MODL/MOD2/MOD3/MOD4: gender/world model-data collections as present; exact
  collection details must remain tied to their authored subrecord ordering.
- BIPL: FormID of an FLST containing ARMA addon references, not a direct ARMA link.
- ETYP: equipment type.
- DATA/DNAM: armor data structures; preserve fields until each byte layout is
  independently verified.
- EITM: optional object effect reference.
- REPL: repair-list reference.
- YNAM/ZNAM: pickup/drop sound references.

EXHAUSTIVE BASE-ESM CENSUS
FalloutNV.esm contains 389 ARMO records. All 389 contain BMDT. 115 contain BIPL;
all 115 BIPL references resolve to FLST records. Expanding those lists produces
247 LNAM entries: 243 resolve to ARMA, one resolves to ARMO, and three remain
unresolved when only the base ESM is loaded. The 389 records expose 259 unique
MODL paths.

ALL-OFFICIAL-MASTER CENSUS
Across FalloutNV.esm and the nine official DLC/pack masters there are 489 ARMO,
161 ARMA, and 506 WEAP records. ARMO exposes 478 MODL, 430 MOD2, 308 MOD3, and
126 MOD4 fields. The collection ordering is the documented male biped, male
world, female biped, female world sequence: MODL, MOD2, MOD3, MOD4. 142 ARMO
BIPL lists expand to 301 LNAM entries; 300 resolve to ARMA and one reference is
unresolved in the combined master set.

The complete NIF census found 946 unique equipment model paths and resolved all
946 through the official BSAs. 758 paths contain skin data and 188 are unskinned.
The generated machine-readable report is under enginedocs/equipment_census/.

RESOURCE MODEL
ARMO should become an ArmorRecord Resource. Its BIPL should resolve to a typed
BipedModelListResource/FLST Resource, whose entries resolve to ArmorAddonRecord
resources. Inventory ownership and currently equipped state belong to separate
runtime components.
