ARMA — Armor Addon record

AUTHORED ROLE
ARMA is a visual armor addon selected through an armor's Biped Model List. It
provides the gender-specific biped/world model data and the slot mask that tells
the actor equipment compositor which body regions it occupies.

CONFIRMED FIELDS
- BMDT: 8-byte biped data; its first uint32 is the biped-slot flag mask.
- MODL/MOD3: biped model collections for the genders as authored by the record
  ordering; MOD2/MOD4 are optional additional world-model collections.
- ETYP: equipment type.
- DATA/DNAM: addon data; preserve unknown fields until their layout is verified.

EXHAUSTIVE BASE-ESM CENSUS
FalloutNV.esm contains 131 ARMA records. All 131 contain BMDT; 129 contain a
MODL. There are 115 unique MODL paths. ARMA records are reached through the
FLST records referenced by ARMO.BIPL; the 115 armor lists contain 243 ARMA
entries resolving to 79 unique ARMA FormIDs.

ALL-OFFICIAL-MASTER CENSUS
Across the ten official masters there are 161 ARMA records. The observed model
collection ordering is MODL (male biped), MOD2 (male world), MOD3 (female
biped), MOD4 (female world), matching the documented collection ordering. The
combined census found 159 MODL fields, 2 MOD2 fields, and 114 MOD3 fields; no
MOD4 field was present on an ARMA record in this shipped dataset. All 946 unique
equipment model paths, including direct ARMO paths and linked ARMA paths, were
resolved successfully through the official BSAs.

NIF SKINNING AND PARTITIONS
The equipment NIFs use the same real BSDismemberSkinInstance path as the nude
humanoid meshes. 758 unique equipment paths contain skin data; the remainder
are unskinned model variants or props. Partition IDs are retained per skin
target and are not renamed or equated to BMDT slot bits. The observed equipment
population includes IDs 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 10, 13, 101, 103, 105, 107, 110,
201, 203, 205, 207, 210, 1000, 3000, 5000, 7000, 10000, 65533, and 65535;
65533 and 65535 remain unknown/sentinel-like values and must be preserved.

COMPOSITION ROLE
An ArmorAddonRecord is not an inventory item by itself. EquipmentState selects
ARMO items, expands their BipedModelList resources, chooses the actor's gender
model collection, and composes the resulting ARMA meshes against the actor's
shared Skeleton3D. BMDT overlap must drive body-part hiding/occupancy; mesh or
node names are not authoritative.
