INFO — Dialogue response record

AUTHORED ROLE
INFO records define dialogue responses. They belong to a DIAL topic and may
reference a speaker, response text, voice data, emotion metadata, and authored
speaker/listener animations.

RELEVANT SUBRECORDS
- ANAM: explicit speaker reference, normally NPC_ or CREA.
- NAM1: response text.
- TRDT: response metadata, including response number, emotion type/value, and
  an optional sound FormID.
- SNAM: speaker animation FormID, referencing an IDLE record.
- LNAM: listener animation FormID, referencing an IDLE record.

TRDT EMOTION DATA
The documented emotion types are Neutral, Anger, Disgust, Fear, Sad, Happy,
Surprise, and Pained. The Use Emotion Animation flag is distinct from the
emotion value; it indicates that the corresponding authored emotion animation
may be used.

VOICE LINKING
The shipped voice files are conventionally named from the quest EditorID,
dialogue EditorID, INFO FormID, and response number, then stored below the
plugin and voice-type directories. The matching .LIP file uses the same stem
as the voice audio file. The TRDT sound FormID is therefore not the only voice
linking mechanism and is absent from most surveyed FalloutNV INFO records.

LOCAL CENSUS
FalloutNV.esm contains 23,247 INFO records and 29,674 response-text/TRDT
entries. The surveyed DLC masters also contain INFO/TRDT response data.
Nonzero TRDT sound FormIDs were uncommon: 171 in FalloutNV.esm, 27 in
DeadMoney.esm, 10 in OldWorldBlues.esm, and 20 in LonesomeRoad.esm; none were
found in the surveyed Honest Hearts or Gun Runners' Arsenal records.

RUNTIME IMPLICATION
INFO is the dialogue-side source for selecting spoken audio, LIP timing,
emotion, and authored gesture animations. It should be resolved into typed
dialogue resources rather than copied into actor-specific variable names.
