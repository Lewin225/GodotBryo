EYES — Eyes record

ROLE
EYES is an independent ESM record with its own FormID. NPC_ ENAM references the
record and the runtime resolves that relationship as a typed resource.

RESOLVED DATA
- FormID and EditorID.
- Display name where present.
- Eye model path.
- Icon path.

RUNTIME MODEL
EYES becomes an EyesRecord resource. NpcRecord.eyes points directly to that
resource rather than duplicating an eyes FormID and model fields on NPCActor.
