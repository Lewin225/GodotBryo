HAIR — Hair record

ROLE
HAIR is an independent ESM record with its own FormID. NPC_ HNAM references the
record; it is not merely a copied model-path string on the NPC node.

RESOLVED DATA
- FormID and EditorID.
- Display name where present.
- Hair model path.
- Icon path.
- Record flags.

RUNTIME MODEL
HAIR becomes a HairRecord resource. NpcRecord.hair points directly to that
resource, preserving the hair record's identity and authoritative fields.

HEAD-SPACE IMPORT FINDING
An exhaustive census of 134 shipped Characters\\Hair and Characters\\Head NIFs
found 40 hair NIFs with the same authored visible-node orientation marker,
approximately -90 degrees X and +/-180 degrees Y. HairFemaleB.NIF is one of
these common cases; it is not a Veronica-specific offset. The standalone hair
mesh is attached beneath the actor's HeadAnims BoneAttachment3D, which already
provides the actor head-space transform. Applying the hair NIF's outer
orientation again produces the observed rotated hair. Head, mouth, eye, and
teeth parts retain their authored mesh transforms; this exception is specific
to unskinned HAIR head-space assembly.
Direct inspection of HairFemaleB.NIF confirmed two NoHat/Hat meshes and zero
skin entries, so it has no vertex weights or bone bindings of its own.

IMPLEMENTATION CONTRACT
When a HairRecord model is assembled under FacialHeadAssembly, preserve its
submesh geometry, materials, morph targets, and Hat/NoHat mesh variants, but
use identity for the standalone hair mesh node transform. The actor's
HeadAnims attachment is the authoritative placement frame.
