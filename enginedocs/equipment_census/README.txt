Official equipment census outputs

Generated by native/tools/equipment_nif_census.cpp.

Scope: FalloutNV.esm and all nine official DLC/pack masters, with all official
BSA archives in fresh_nv_copy/Data/.

Files:
- equipment_records.csv: ARMO/ARMA model fields, source plugin, FormIDs, and
  BMDT slot masks.
- equipment_nifs.csv: resolved NIF submeshes, skin targets, bone counts, and
  BSDismember partition IDs. Nude RACE body paths are included as source_kind
  nude_body.
- summary.txt: aggregate counts and resolver status.

The CSVs contain only record identifiers, paths, and extracted metadata. No
original game assets are copied into the repository.
